Amazon FBA Calculator

Referral, fulfillment and storage — modeled per unit.

Amazon FBA profitability comes down to three costs: the referral fee (typically 15% of the total sales price), a per-unit fulfillment fee based on size and weight, and a per-unit storage fee. Sellers frequently underestimate the last two.

Set the fulfillment fee to match your product's size tier and Season, enter your landed cost, and see profit per unit with a break-even price.

Formula

FBA cost = 15% referral (min $0.30/unit) + fulfillment fee + storage fee

Amazon's referral fee is a percentage of the total sales price and has a per-item minimum for most categories. Fulfillment and storage fees depend on dimensions, weight and time of year, so use Amazon's fee schedule for your exact ASIN.

How to calculate it

  1. Enter the sale price buyers pay.
  2. Set the referral percentage and minimum for your category.
  3. Enter the FBA fulfillment fee for your size tier and the storage fee per unit.
  4. Add your landed cost (product + inbound freight) as the item cost.
  5. Check profit per unit and the break-even price before sourcing or repricing.

FAQ

How much is the Amazon referral fee?

Most categories are 15% of the total sales price, with a per-item minimum around $0.30. Some categories, like consumer electronics, are lower.

How is the FBA fulfillment fee set?

It depends on the product's size tier and weight, and it changes with Amazon's rate cards. Enter the figure from Amazon's fee schedule for your ASIN.

Does FBA storage apply to every unit?

Storage is charged on the inventory Amazon holds, by volume and season. Allocating storage per unit sold is a useful approximation.

What sale price should I pick?

Start from the break-even price and add your target profit per unit. Remember that Amazon ads, returns and coupons can reduce the final margin.
