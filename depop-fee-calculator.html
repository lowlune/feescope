Depop Fee Calculator

The 10% selling fee plus processing — what you actually keep on Depop.

Depop charges a selling fee that is a percentage of the item price plus the shipping you charge, and payment processing is added per order. Together they decide whether a listing is worth running.

This calculator itemizes both, subtracts your item cost and actual shipping, and shows the price at which profit hits zero. Every rate is editable because Depop fees and payment providers vary by country.

Formula

Depop fees = 10% selling fee + (~3.3% + $0.45) payment processing

The selling fee applies to the item price plus shipping charged, so offering free shipping does not remove the fee on that amount. Payment processing is charged per order. U.S. sellers using Depop Payments are the default; adjust the rates for your country and provider.

How to calculate it

  1. Enter the item price and the shipping you charge the buyer.
  2. Enter your item cost and the actual shipping you pay.
  3. Adjust the selling and processing rates for your country and payment method.
  4. Check that your price sits above break-even with the margin you want.

FAQ

What is Depop's selling fee?

Depop's U.S. selling fee is about 10% of the item price plus shipping charged, with payment processing of roughly 3.3% + $0.45 per order. Rates differ by country.

Does Depop charge a fee on shipping?

Yes. The selling fee is calculated on the item price plus the shipping you charge the buyer, so free shipping still incurs the fee on that amount.

Does Depop charge payment processing separately?

Yes. Depop Payments processing is charged per order on top of the selling fee. The default here reflects a common U.S. rate — edit it to match your account.

How do I price for a Depop profit target?

Use the break-even price as your floor, then add your target margin. Re-check whenever Depop updates its fees or you switch payment providers.
