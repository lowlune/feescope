Poshmark Fee Calculator

See the 20% commission — and your real payout — before you accept an offer.

Poshmark keeps its fee structure simple: it takes a 20% commission on sales of $15 or more, and a flat $2.95 on sales under $15. The commission is based on the item sale price, and the buyer pays shipping separately.

Because Poshmark fees come off the top, a low-cost item can leave very little behind. Enter your cost and price to see net payout, profit and the break-even price where the sale stops making money.

Formula

Poshmark commission = 20% of item price ($15+), or a flat $2.95 (under $15)

Poshmark does not charge a separate payment-processing fee to the seller, and shipping is paid by the buyer, so the item price is the fee base. The $15 threshold matters: just below it a flat $2.95 applies, which is a much larger share of a cheap item than 20% would be.

How to calculate it

  1. Enter the item price you plan to list or accept.
  2. Enter what the item cost you (and any outbound cost you absorb).
  3. Set the commission rate and the flat-fee threshold if Poshmark changes them.
  4. Compare the break-even price to your offer before you accept or make one.

FAQ

How much does Poshmark take per sale?

Poshmark charges a 20% commission on sales of $15 or more and a flat $2.95 on sales under $15. The commission is based on the item sale price.

Does Poshmark charge fees on shipping?

No. On Poshmark the buyer pays shipping, and the commission is calculated on the item sale price, not on shipping.

Why is my Poshmark profit so low on cheap items?

Under $15 the flat $2.95 fee can be a large share of a cheap item. Either bundle items or price above the threshold where the percentage fee is proportionally smaller.

Does Poshmark charge payment processing?

Poshmark does not charge the seller a separate payment-processing fee the way Etsy does. Its commission is the main selling cost.
