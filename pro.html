FAQ

Do I need Excel or Google Sheets?

No. FeeScope Pro is a single self-contained HTML file that runs offline in any modern browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

How many products can I import?

Thousands of rows, limited only by your device and browser memory.

Can I edit the fee rates?

Yes — every rate is configurable per marketplace, and you can switch a product between marketplaces to compare.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your costs and prices never leave your computer.

Do I get updates?

Yes. Free updates to the same download — re-download from your purchase receipt.
